Frequently Asked Questions

Honest, plain-language answers about our services, fees, and what to expect — based directly on our consultancy agreement.

Our Services

What We Help With

We provide advisory and administrative support for four things: (1) university admission guidance — choosing programs, course matching, and submitting your application via the Universitaly portal; (2) pre-enrollment & documentation — compiling academic papers, translations, and CIMEA/DOV statements; (3) visa guidance — reviewing your checklist requirements; and (4) general advice on the Permesso di Soggiorno (residence permit) process within 8 days of arriving in Italy.

Scholarship and regional grant programs (like DSU or EDISU) are run entirely by Italian regional bodies, not by us. They are not part of our paid service scope, and we do not guarantee scholarship outcomes. If you'd like, we can point you toward the official portals so you can apply directly.

CIMEA (Centro di Informazione sulla Mobilità e le Equivalenze Accademiche) issues the Dichiarazione di Valore (DOV) — the official Italian recognition of your existing academic qualifications. Universities and the consulate require it before enrollment. We assist with compiling and submitting the right documents; the CIMEA/pre-screening fee itself is paid directly by you.

No. We act in an advisory and administrative capacity only. The final decision on admission, scholarships, or a Type-D study visa always rests with the respective Italian university and the Italian diplomatic authorities — we do not and cannot guarantee any outcome.

Fees & Payment

Costs, Payments & Refunds

Our total service fee is a fixed PKR 100,000, split into two installments: PKR 50,000 payable upon signing the service agreement, and PKR 50,000 payable once you secure university admission/acceptance. There are no hidden charges — the fee structure is fixed and published upfront.

No. Our service fee excludes third-party costs — university application fees, pre-screening fees (e.g. CIMEA), embassy/consulate visa fees, health insurance premiums, and translation costs. These are paid directly by you to the relevant institution, consulate, or provider.

Yes — the initial PKR 50,000 advance is refundable if university admission is not secured through no fault of yours. However, service fees become strictly non-refundable once admission is granted, or if your visa application is rejected due to false documents, insufficient funds, or missing your appointment.

Your Responsibilities

What We Expect From You

You agree to provide accurate, truthful, and authentic documents and personal information; pay all official fees directly to universities, consulates, or government portals; and attend every mandatory in-person appointment at the Italian Embassy/Consulate.

Our service fee becomes non-refundable if your visa is rejected because of false documents, lack of funds, or failure to attend your appointment. This is why honesty and preparation matter — we're only able to guide you well when the information you give us is accurate.
